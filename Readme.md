I work at the intersection of chemical engineering, plant operations, and data-driven modeling. My focus is on analyzing and simulating industrial processes using Python, process data, and machine learning. I develop models for fault detection and anomaly identification using historical plant and DCS data, and build time-series forecasting models, including LSTM-based approaches, to predict future process outputs such as purity and quality. My goal is to apply chemical engineering fundamentals together with data and AI models to improve process understanding, operational efficiency, and decision-making in industrial environments.
